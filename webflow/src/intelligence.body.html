Trivium was built by brokers with decades of multifamily experience and relationships across the markets we serve. Now we’re building the intelligence infrastructure to make that experience more powerful.

What We’re Building

From information to intelligence.

Knowing what traded is useful. Understanding why it traded, who bought it, what else they own, where capital is moving, and which buyers are most likely to transact next is intelligence.

Trivium is building systems that connect those signals. Instead of market knowledge living across spreadsheets, inboxes, databases, and individual brokers’ heads, we’re creating a shared intelligence layer across the firm.

01 / Connected Ownership Intelligence

A continuously improving view of multifamily ownership connecting properties, entities, principals, portfolios, transactions, and relationships.

02 / AI-Powered Research

Tools that move brokers from raw information to actionable intelligence faster.

03 / Buyer & Capital Intelligence

Tracking buyer behavior to understand who is deploying capital, where they’re buying, and what fits.

04 / Broker Intelligence

Giving Trivium brokers access to institutional knowledge historically held by individual senior producers.

05 / Market Intelligence

Tracking what traded, where, at what unit count, who bought it, and how activity is changing.
